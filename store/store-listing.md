Meteor Match

## Description (943/4000)

A same-game puzzle with meteor tiles, ported from the AsteroidOS smartwatch game.

Tap a group of two or more connected tiles of the same colour to blast it. The tiles above fall into the gap, and empty columns close up. A group of N tiles scores (N-1) squared, so big groups pay far more than small ones. If falling tiles form a new group of three or more in the colour you just cleared, it blasts too and scores again. Clear the whole board for a bonus of 100.

The board is larger than the screen: drag to pan, and on a big match the view zooms out to show it all.

Your game is saved after every move, even if the app is closed mid-move. Swipe the app away and its cover shows the board as you left it.

Needs no permissions. Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/moWerk/asteroid-meteormatch
The SailfishOS port was written with an AI assistant, directed and play-tested by the author; the title art is AI-generated. Details in the repository.
